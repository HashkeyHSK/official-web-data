## [Announcement] HSK Staking Phase 1 Settlement & Compensation

Dear HSK Community,
<br></br>
Phase 1 of HSK Staking has now matured. Users can proceed with unstaking as per the standard process — there is no need to wait for the compensation payout.
<br></br>
Regarding the Settlement of This Phase
Following a thorough review, we have identified a technical issue with the yield calculation formula for this phase. As a result, some users may have received a total amount lower than the expected settlement amount based on the campaign's compensation standards upon unstaking. This shortfall may involve both a principal shortfall and yield that failed to be credited properly.
<br></br>
Compensation Standards
<br></br>
This compensation is based on a 5.5% annualized yield rate, prorated according to the actual staking period selected by the user:
- 365-day lock-up: 5.50%
- 180-day lock-up: 2.75%
- 90-day lock-up: 1.38%
- 30-day lock-up: 0.69%

Other staking scenarios: Prorated based on the staking type and actual number of staking days.
<br></br>
The compensation amount for each individual transaction is calculated as follows:
<br></br>
Compensation Amount = Principal + Target Yield (calculated per the standards above) − Actual Amount Received or Currently Claimable On-Chain
If the calculated result is greater than zero, we will compensate the difference. If the result is less than or equal to zero, no compensation is required.
<br></br>
Compensation Arrangements
The compensation covers:
- The principal shortfall that was not properly credited;
- The yield calculated per the standards above that failed to be credited properly.

Compensation will be credited directly to users' original staking addresses without the need for a separate application, and is expected to be completed by the end of November 2026. Please ensure that you still have normal access to and use of your original staking address by that time.
<br></br>
Unstaking Arrangements
<br></br>
The unstaking process remains open as normal. Users do not need to wait for the compensation to complete their unstaking. The difference eligible for compensation will be distributed collectively within the timeframe stated above.
<br></br>
Please note: 
<br></br>
We will never ask you to transfer funds in the name of compensation, nor will we ask for your private keys, seed phrases, or verification codes. Please stay vigilant against scams.
<br></br>
We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience and confusion caused by this settlement issue. Thank you for your understanding and trust. We will continue to optimize our settlement mechanisms and verification processes to enhance the security and transparency of our products.
<br></br>

## 關於 HSK Staking 第一期到期結算及收益補償的公告
各位社區夥伴：
<br></br>
HSK Staking 第一期現已到期。用戶可以按照原流程正常發起解押，無需等待補償發放。
<br></br>
關於本期結算情況
<br></br>
經核查，本期收益結算公式存在技術問題，導致部分用戶解押後實際領取的總金額低於按照活動補償標準應到帳的金額，可能同時涉及本金差額及未能正常到帳的收益。
<br></br>
補償標準
<br></br>
本次補償以 5.5% 年化收益率為基準，並根據用戶實際選擇的質押期限折算對應收益：
- 365 天鎖倉：5.50%
- 180 天鎖倉：2.75%
- 90 天鎖倉：1.38%
- 30 天鎖倉：0.69%


其他質押情形：根據質押類型及實際質押天數折算
<br></br>
單筆補償金額按照以下方式計算：
<br></br>
補償金額 = 本金 + 按上述標準計算的目標收益 − 實際已領取金額或當前鏈上可領取金額
<br></br>
如計算結果大於零，我們將按該差額進行補償；如計算結果小於或等於零，則無需補償。
<br></br>
補償安排
<br></br>
補償範圍包括：
<br></br>
1. 未能正常到帳的本金差額；
2. 按上述標準計算、但未能正常到帳的收益。

補償將直接發放至用戶原質押地址，無需提交申請，預計於 2026 年 11 月底前完成。請確保屆時仍可正常訪問和使用原質押地址。
<br></br>
解质押安排
<br></br>
解质押流程正常開放，用戶無需等待補償，可以按照原流程完成解押。符合補償標準的差額將在上述時間內統一發放。
<br></br>
請注意：官方不會以補償名義要求您轉帳，也不會索取私鑰、助記詞或驗證碼，請謹防詐騙。
對於本次結算問題給大家帶來的困擾與不便，我們深表歉意。感謝大家的理解與信任，我們將持續優化結算機制與核驗流程，提升產品的安全性和透明度。
